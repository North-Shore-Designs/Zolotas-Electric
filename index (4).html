About Zolotas Electric

Our Story

A second-generation electrical business, built on trust

Zolotas Electric is a second-generation, family-owned and operated electrical contracting company serving homes and businesses across the North Shore of Boston. What began decades ago as one electrician's practice has grown into a full-service operation covering installations, service calls, and sub contracting — without losing the personal, family-run feel that started it all.

We offer a range of electrical services and installations, focusing on quality workmanship and satisfied customers. That's the whole philosophy: do the job right, and the relationship takes care of itself — whether you're a homeowner, a landlord managing a portfolio, or a general contractor who needs an electrician they can trust on site.

Today the business is run by master electricians John Zolotas and Greg Zolotas, covering Essex County, MA and the North Shore of Boston.
